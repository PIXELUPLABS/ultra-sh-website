Why Ultra?

UN.01 Hosts - Dedicated AMD EPYC 4005 servers with NVMe storage and dual 10G uplinks. Contention monitoring runs on every host, and spare capacity is held in reserve. Real people on duty 24/7 investigate hardware, hypervisor and network issues, live-migrate your VM to spare capacity and recover it after a failure.

UN.02 VMs - Unmanaged cloud VMs with full root access on dedicated cores. You run the OS and apps, we operate the infrastructure underneath. 0 GB of RAM is shared with your neighbours, so a busy host never competes for your CPU or memory, and every VM runs on NVMe storage.

UN.03 People - Real people on support 24/7, from the team behind Ultra.cc. When a fan, PSU or DIMM fails, an engineer checks the host instead of your ticket waiting in a queue. One flat monthly price per VM in USD, in 5 cities across the USA, Europe and Asia.
